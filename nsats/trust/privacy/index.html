Introduction

National Scientific AI & Technical Systems Ltd, trading as NSATS Ltd (we or NSATS), is incorporated in the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC). We are the controller of the personal information described in this notice.

Learn more

This notice explains how we collect, use and share information when you visit our website or interact with us. It covers accounts, enquiries, resource requests, newsletters, recruitment, supplier submissions and related administration. A separately identified employer or contracting company manages its own employment or contractual records. Relevant forms and the Recruitment privacy notice provide further information about specific activities.

Personal information we collect and use

We collect information from you, your browser and authorised business contacts. We also receive relevant information from incident reporters and infrastructure providers.

Learn more

Required fields are marked. Without the information needed for a request, we may be unable to fulfil it. Other fields are optional. Please do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive information through general forms. We use the information needed to answer requests, provide resources, manage access and handle business relationships, applications and reports.

Your account

We use your contact and authentication information to create, operate and protect your account. This includes your name, email address and access records.

Learn more

We use these details to verify your email and administer permissions for requested resources. Email verification does not grant access to separately restricted financial or licensed material. Financial access requires a separate decision. We use the identity, organisation, purpose and supporting information you provide to assess that request. Our account records remain while your account is active. The retention table explains closure and unverified-registration periods.

NSATS website

We use website information to provide access, operate and secure the site, and adapt pages to your device and language. Our own optional analytics helps us understand use, measure performance and improve content and services.

Learn more

Website information includes:

  • Pages and resources opened, time spent on pages and links selected.
  • The referring page address and general location derived from your IP address.
  • Your IP address, browser type and browser request headers (technical details your browser sends with each page request).
  • Operating system, device type and version, and language settings.
  • Dates and times, error reports, access events and other interactions.
  • Your account identifier when signed in, site identifiers and browser signatures.

Browser signatures combine technical characteristics that distinguish browsers or devices. Optional analytics uses website activity, technical information and site identifiers only with your consent. We operate that analytics ourselves and do not send it to an external analytics service. We do not use third-party marketing or advertising trackers and do not sell analytics data.

Enquiries and telephone calls

We use your details to answer enquiries, supply requested resources and handle privacy requests or reports. Calls are answered for us by a service provider and may be recorded so your enquiry can be passed on and answered.

Learn more

You may provide your name, business contact details, organisation, request and supporting information through forms or correspondence. For protected resources, we also use email verification and access records to deliver material and protect access. Requesting a publication does not subscribe you to the newsletter. Call information and recordings follow the enquiries retention period below.

When you submit a privacy, security or conduct report, we use the relevant details and evidence to handle it. Share only what is needed for the matter. Privacy requests and reporting cases have their own retention periods in the table.

Newsletter

We send our newsletter only after a separate opt-in. We do not use your personal information for other advertising or unsolicited promotional outreach.

Learn more

Newsletter consent is separate from account registration and analytics. We email a confirmation link when you request a subscription. Ignore that message if you did not request it. Unconfirmed requests expire after 30 days.

Use the unsubscribe link or contact us to stop the newsletter. We retain a minimal suppression record to respect your choice and review it annually. Unsubscribing does not stop essential messages about an enquiry, account or service you requested.

Contractual relationships

We use business contact and supporting information to manage licensing and supplier discussions and relationships. The relevant agreement identifies the contracting entity.

Learn more

We use your organisation details, requests and qualification information to assess and administer the proposed relationship. Supplier registration does not guarantee an order. If an agreement follows, relevant contractual and invoice records follow the applicable retention requirements. Enquiries that do not lead to a supplier engagement follow the enquiries period in the table.

Protecting you and NSATS

We analyse technical information and activity to authenticate access, detect attacks, prevent fraud and investigate incidents. Necessary security monitoring continues whether you accept or reject optional analytics.

Learn more

We correlate IP addresses, request headers, site identifiers, browser signatures and activity patterns to detect bots and malicious requests. Automated controls may challenge, limit or block requests. Contact us if you believe a restriction is mistaken. Security information is used for security and is not automatically reused for optional analytics. We handle reportable breaches under the notification requirements that apply.

Recruitment

We use application details, qualifications and relevant communications to assess candidates and arrange recruitment. Our Recruitment privacy notice explains the information collected, access, retention, legal bases and your rights. Each vacancy identifies the recruiting employer.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, local storage and similar technologies to operate the site and remember choices. Optional analytics stays off until you allow it.

Learn more

These technologies include browser or device characteristics that help distinguish one browser or device from another. Necessary functions support sign-in, authorised access, saved privacy preferences and security.

On your first visit, choose Accept analytics, Reject analytics or Settings. We remember your choice for 6 months, then ask again. A material change may require an earlier new choice. Open Analytics settings at any time to change your choice or withdraw consent as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal stops optional analytics in that browser. Rejecting analytics leaves public content and necessary protection available.

Browser controls can remove cookies or block stored information. This may remove a saved preference or interrupt sign-in. Deleting cookies alone may not stop browser-signature processing, so use Analytics settings to withdraw optional analytics consent. Contact our privacy team about information already collected. The Cookies and analytics choices page explains these technologies and their retention periods.

Children

Our forms, accounts and subscriptions are for adults aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly accept personal information from or about children.

Learn more

Do not submit children’s information through this website. Contact our privacy team if you believe it has been supplied. We remove it unless a legal duty requires us to keep necessary information. Routine technical logs may still be generated during a child’s visit.

International transfers

Personal information may be processed in, or accessed from, the countries where NSATS and its service providers operate.

Learn more

We transfer personal information to another country only where one of the following applies: the country is recognised as providing an adequate level of protection; standard data protection clauses approved by the regulator are in place with the recipient; or the law otherwise permits the transfer, for example where you have explicitly consented or the transfer is needed for a contract with you.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete, restrict or provide a portable copy of your personal information. You can withdraw consent without affecting processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Your right to object

You can object to processing based on our legitimate interests for reasons relating to your situation. We stop unless we demonstrate overriding compelling legitimate grounds or need the information for legal claims. You can object to direct marketing at any time; we then stop that use.

Exercising your rights

Use the privacy enquiry form on this website, free of charge and without creating an account. You can also email privacy.global@nsats.com or write to Gate Village 6, PO Box 482007, DIFC, Dubai, UAE.

Learn more

You do not need a newsletter subscription to make a request. We may reasonably verify your identity and explain any lawful limits on your request. DIFC data protection law applies to how we handle your personal information, wherever that takes place. Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have additional rights.

Complaints

You may complain to the DIFC Commissioner of Data Protection. You do not have to complain to us first.
