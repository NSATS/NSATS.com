Privacy notice

This notice explains how NSATS Ltd processes personal data when you use this website, contact NSATS, create a MyNSATS account, subscribe to NSATS updates or register as a supplier. NSATS Ltd processes this data in accordance with the DIFC Data Protection Law (DIFC Law No. 5 of 2020); where people in Switzerland, the European Union or the United States are concerned, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection, the EU General Data Protection Regulation and applicable state laws also apply, as the regional sections below explain. NSATS Ltd will comply with all national and other regulations that apply to its processing at any time.

Recruitment is covered by separate notices: the DIFC recruitment privacy notice for roles based in the DIFC and general careers enquiries, and the Swiss recruitment privacy notice for roles based in Switzerland.

Your right to object

Where NSATS Ltd processes your personal data on the basis of its legitimate interests, you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation, and NSATS Ltd will stop unless it has compelling grounds to continue. You may object at any time to the use of your personal data for direct marketing, and NSATS Ltd will stop. To object, use the privacy enquiries form.

Who is responsible

National Scientific AI & Technical Systems Ltd (NSATS Ltd), registered in the Dubai International Financial Centre, is the controller of the personal data covered by this notice.

NSATS Dubai office handles personal data of people outside Switzerland and the European Union. Postal address: 482007, Gate Village 6, DIFC, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.

NSATS Swiss office handles personal data of people in Switzerland and the European Union. Postal address: PO1, Geneva 1, Switzerland.

NSATS USA office is a branch office. Postal address: Suite 1201, New York, NY 10001, USA. Personal data of people in the United States is handled by the NSATS Dubai office.

For people in Switzerland and the European Union, the email contact is privacy.eu@nsats.com; this mailbox is hosted in Switzerland. For everyone else, the email contact is privacy.global@nsats.com; this mailbox is hosted in the DIFC.

NSATS Ltd has appointed a data protection officer. Messages for the data protection officer must be made only through the privacy enquiries form, choosing the office that handles your data. Questions about this notice and requests to exercise your rights may be made through that form, the email address for your region, or by post to the office that handles your data.

What NSATS Ltd collects and why

Website visits. When you visit the website, NSATS Ltd and its hosting provider process technical data: your IP address, browser and device type, the pages requested and the time of each request. NSATS Ltd uses this data to deliver the website, keep it secure and investigate faults. The lawful basis is NSATS Ltd’s legitimate interest in operating a secure and working website. Cookies and similar technologies are explained in the cookie notice.

Enquiries. When you use an enquiry form, NSATS Ltd collects your name, email address, organisation if you give it, and the content of your enquiry, including any reference or subject you choose. This includes licensing enquiries and requests for the Master Licence Agreement (MLA) or an NDA, including an NDA for a relationship other than licensing. NSATS Ltd uses this data to answer you and to keep a record of the exchange. Where your enquiry concerns a possible agreement, such as a licence or a supply, the lawful basis is the steps taken at your request before entering into a contract; otherwise it is NSATS Ltd’s legitimate interest in answering enquiries and managing its business relationships.

Security and business-conduct reports. When you report a security issue or a business-conduct concern, NSATS Ltd uses the information you provide to investigate and respond. The lawful basis is NSATS Ltd’s legitimate interest in protecting its systems and in maintaining proper conduct in its business relationships, and compliance with applicable law where the law requires NSATS Ltd to act.

MyNSATS accounts. When you create an account, NSATS Ltd collects your name, email address, organisation if you give it, and a password. NSATS Ltd also records whether your email address is verified and which access has been approved for you. NSATS Ltd uses this data to provide the account under the terms that apply to it. The lawful basis is the performance of that contract.

Financial-access requests. When you request access to financial information, NSATS Ltd collects the details in the request, including your organisation, role and the purpose of the request. NSATS Ltd uses them to decide whether to grant the request and to prepare any agreement. The lawful bases are the steps taken at your request before entering into a contract and NSATS Ltd’s legitimate interest in controlling who receives confidential company information. Requests for an NDA go through the licensing enquiry.

NSATS updates. If you subscribe, NSATS Ltd uses your email address to send news and technology updates. The lawful basis is your consent, which you give by selecting the subscription box. You may withdraw consent at any time through the unsubscribe link in each email or the unsubscribe page, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before you withdraw.

A subscription is confirmed through a link sent to the email address given and is not active until that link is used. NSATS sends the updates from its own system, hosted in Switzerland for subscribers in Switzerland and the European Union, and in the DIFC for everyone else. NSATS asks subscribers to renew their consent every twelve months and stops sending updates if they do not. Every email identifies NSATS as the sender, carries NSATS’s postal address and includes an unsubscribe link that takes effect promptly.

Suppliers. When you register as a supplier or send a procurement enquiry, NSATS Ltd collects your organisation’s details and the name and contact details of its contact person, together with the information provided about goods, services and experience. NSATS Ltd uses this data to assess suppliers and to manage supply relationships. The lawful bases are the steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, the performance of a supply contract and NSATS Ltd’s legitimate interest in managing its supply chain. Where your organisation gives NSATS Ltd your details as its contact, your organisation is the source of that data.

Privacy requests and legal obligations. NSATS Ltd uses the information in a privacy request to answer it and to keep a record that it has done so, as the law requires. NSATS Ltd may also use personal data where it is necessary to comply with applicable law or to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim.

Providing personal data is voluntary. Fields marked as required are needed for NSATS Ltd to answer an enquiry, provide an account or consider a request; without them, NSATS Ltd cannot do so. Do not include information about health, religion or other sensitive matters, or confidential technical information, unless NSATS Ltd has asked for it.

Who receives the data

Personal data is seen only by the NSATS Ltd staff who need it for the purposes above, and by the service provider that hosts the website’s static pages under contract and on NSATS Ltd’s instructions. NSATS Ltd may share personal data with its professional advisers, such as lawyers and auditors, and with authorities where the law requires it. NSATS Ltd does not sell personal data and does not give it to others for their own marketing. That provider is GitHub, Inc., described below. NSATS operates the forms, accounts and email services itself, through the office that handles your data.

Where the data is held

The website’s static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., in the United States. GitHub records visitors’ IP addresses and request details for security. For people in Switzerland and the European Union, this is a transfer to the United States. GitHub processes this data under its data protection agreement with NSATS, which includes the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. A copy of the safeguards is available through the privacy enquiries form.

The forms, served from forms.nsats.com and api.nsats.com, and the databases, storage, mailboxes, backups and the system that sends NSATS updates are self-hosted by NSATS on its own systems. Personal data of people in Switzerland and the European Union is held in Switzerland and handled by the NSATS Swiss office. Personal data of everyone else is held in the DIFC and handled by the NSATS Dubai office. Data held on these systems is not moved between the offices.

NSATS Ltd transfers personal data out of the DIFC only as the DIFC Data Protection Law permits, using the safeguards that law requires, and will update this notice before any new transfer takes place.

Personal data held on NSATS’s own systems in Switzerland is not disclosed abroad. If a disclosure abroad becomes necessary, NSATS Ltd will make it only as the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection permits, with the safeguards it requires, and will update this notice before the disclosure takes place.

How long NSATS Ltd keeps the data

NSATS Ltd keeps personal data only for as long as it needs it for the purpose for which it was collected, and then deletes it.

NSATS Ltd keeps:

  • website server logs for 30 days, unless they are needed to investigate a security incident;
  • enquiries for 12 months after the last exchange;
  • signed NDAs and licences for as long as the agreement’s obligations last, as specified and agreed by both parties, and afterwards for as long as needed for legal claims;
  • security and business-conduct reports for two years after the matter is closed;
  • MyNSATS account data while the account is open, and deletes it within 30 days after the account is closed;
  • financial-access requests for two years after the decision or, if an agreement results, for the life of that agreement;
  • your email address for NSATS updates until you unsubscribe;
  • supplier records for the duration of the supply relationship and then for as long as accounting and tax law requires;
  • records of privacy requests for two years after the response.

If a legal claim has been made, NSATS Ltd keeps the data needed for that claim until the claim is resolved.

Automated decisions

NSATS Ltd does not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

Your rights

Subject to the conditions of the DIFC Data Protection Law, you may request access to your personal data, its correction or deletion, the restriction of its processing and a copy of it in a portable format, and you may object to its processing. Where processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time. Requests should be made through the privacy enquiries form or the email address for your region. NSATS Ltd answers requests free of charge and within one month, except where the DIFC Data Protection Law provides otherwise.

You may lodge a complaint with the DIFC Commissioner of Data Protection.

People in Switzerland and the European Union

Personal data handled on NSATS’s own systems for people in Switzerland and the European Union is handled by the NSATS Swiss office and held in Switzerland. GitHub’s processing of website visitor data in the United States is described under “Where the data is held”.

The Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection applies. Where EU data protection law applies, you have the rights it gives to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability and objection, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. NSATS Ltd answers requests under EU data protection law without undue delay and within one month, except where that law permits an extension; NSATS Ltd will notify you of any such extension within that month. Requests for information under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection are generally answered within 30 days.

For this region, questions and requests may be made through privacy.eu@nsats.com, the privacy enquiries form, or by post to the NSATS Swiss office. Messages for the data protection officer must be made only through the privacy enquiries form.

You may report a concern to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner. Where EU data protection law applies, you may complain to a supervisory authority, including in the member state where you live or work.

People in the United States

Personal data handled on NSATS’s own systems for people in the United States is handled by the NSATS Dubai office and held in the DIFC.
