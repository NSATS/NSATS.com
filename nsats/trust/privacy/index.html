Privacy notice

Effective date: 10 November 2026. National Scientific AI & Technical Systems Ltd, trading as NSATS Ltd (NSATS, we or us), is incorporated in the Dubai International Financial Centre and is the controller of the website personal data described in this notice. Contact our Data Protection Officer and privacy team at privacy.global@nsats.com or Gate Village 6, PO Box 482007, DIFC, Dubai, UAE.

This notice covers website visits, enquiries, accounts, resource requests, newsletter subscriptions, recruitment, supplier submissions and related administration. A separately identified employer or contracting company is responsible for its own employment or contractual records.

Information we collect and why

We collect information you provide through forms, accounts and correspondence, including your name, business contact details, organisation, request or application, and relevant supporting information. We use it to respond, provide requested services and resources, administer access and business relationships, consider applications, and handle reports or privacy requests.

We collect technical information needed to deliver and protect requested services: IP addresses, dates and times, browser request headers, requested pages and resources, browser and operating-system information, session cookies, and access and security events.

We receive information from you, your browser, authorised business contacts and, where relevant, incident reporters or infrastructure providers. Required fields are marked. Without the information needed for a request, we may be unable to fulfil it. Please do not send passwords or unrelated sensitive information through general forms.

Security monitoring

We use technical information to operate and protect our infrastructure, authenticate access, investigate incidents and prevent abuse. Access controls and request limits may refuse a request. Contact us if you believe a restriction is mistaken.

Necessary security monitoring continues when optional analytics is declined. Information collected for security is used for that purpose and is not automatically repurposed for optional audience analytics.

Analytics and newsletter

Optional audience analytics is disabled on this website. We do not use advertising trackers or sell analytics data. See Cookies and preferences for browser controls.

We do not use personal information for advertising or unsolicited promotional outreach. We respond to enquiries and provide requested services. We send our newsletter only after a separate opt-in; use the unsubscribe link or contact us to stop it. Newsletter consent is separate from analytics and account registration.

Legal bases

Where a lawful basis is required, we rely on legitimate interests to protect our systems, respond to business enquiries and administer business relationships; performance of a contract or steps you request towards one where applicable; legal obligations where relevant; and consent for optional analytics and the newsletter. Recruitment-specific information appears in the Recruitment privacy notice.

Recipients and processing locations

Application data is hosted on OVHcloud VPS infrastructure in Singapore. Backups are held in DIFC, United Arab Emirates. Authorised NSATS personnel in the United Arab Emirates, Switzerland, the United States and Singapore can access information for their work. This is central hosting with authorised international access, not a promise that information stays in a visitor’s country.

Our public pages are delivered through GitHub Pages, which records visitor IP addresses for security. We operate our forms, accounts, email and application services internally. OVHcloud and GitHub remain infrastructure providers; internally operated services do not mean that no service provider processes data. Internet email also passes through the recipient’s email service.

We disclose information only as needed to authorised personnel, relevant NSATS entities, infrastructure providers, professional advisers, or authorities and other recipients where legally required or necessary to establish or defend rights. The employer or contracting entity is identified in the relevant vacancy or agreement. Access is limited to the purpose for which information is needed.

International processing is subject to the applicable data-protection requirements. Where additional transfer safeguards are required, they must cover the relevant recipient and processing; these may include recognised adequacy decisions or approved contractual safeguards. Contact our privacy team for information about the arrangements applicable to your data and copies of relevant safeguards, subject to lawful redactions.

Children

Our submission services, accounts and subscriptions are for adults aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly accept personal information from or about children. Do not submit it through this website. If you believe it has been provided, contact our privacy team so we can remove it, subject to any legal duty to retain necessary information. Routine technical logs may be generated when someone visits; a visit alone does not tell us their age.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact our privacy team. We may reasonably verify your identity and will explain any lawful limitation. You do not need an account or newsletter subscription to make a request.

You may complain to the DIFC Commissioner of Data Protection. Where relevant law applies, you may also approach an appropriate EEA supervisory authority, the UK Information Commissioner, the Swiss FDPIC, Singapore’s PDPC or another competent local authority. You do not have to complain to us first.

DIFC law applies to our DIFC processing. EU GDPR, UK GDPR and PECR, the Swiss FADP, Singapore PDPA and other applicable laws provide additional protections where their scope applies. Your rights are not determined solely by our hosting or contact location. We handle reportable breaches under the applicable notification requirements and update this notice when our practices change.
