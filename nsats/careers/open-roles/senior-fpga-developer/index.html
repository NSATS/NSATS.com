Senior FPGA Developer

Lead the design and verification of programmable hardware at NSATS, including the embedded software that controls it. The role uses field-programmable gate arrays (FPGAs), chips whose logic can be configured to process data and communicate with memory and other systems.

Vacancy reference: NSATS-CS-02 · Area: Computing and software · Location: Dubai, United Arab Emirates (on-site) · Employment type: Full-time · Closing date: Open until filled

About the role

You will take designs from decisions about their architecture through implementation, verification and initial laboratory testing. This includes designing the logic that processes data and controls its movement, implementing high-speed interfaces and integrating custom hardware design blocks with other subsystems. You will investigate how the programmable logic and embedded software work together, maintain the engineering documentation and guide junior developers through design and verification problems.

What you will do

  • Contribute to architecture and design decisions, then carry them through implementation, verification and initial laboratory testing.
  • Design and verify data-path and control-path logic using SystemVerilog or Verilog.
  • Implement interfaces including PCIe, Ethernet, DDR memory and AXI within FPGA designs.
  • Integrate custom hardware design blocks with other subsystems using the FPGA design toolchain.
  • Carry out pre-silicon verification and emulation to examine design behaviour before lab testing.
  • Lead initial laboratory testing and investigate interactions between embedded software and programmable logic.
  • Document the architecture, implementation and verification work.
  • Guide junior developers in their design work and in investigating verification failures.

What you will bring

  • A Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in computer or electrical engineering.
  • Extensive professional FPGA design experience, including at least one completed project on an AMD Versal Series Gen 2 or AMD UltraScale+ device.
  • Expert knowledge of SystemVerilog or Verilog, synchronous design and timing closure for high-speed logic.
  • Knowledge of PCIe, AXI, DDR and Ethernet interfaces.

Relevant device families include Versal AI Edge Series Gen 2, Versal Prime Series Gen 2, Versal Premium Series Gen 2, Zynq UltraScale+ MPSoC, Kintex UltraScale+ and Virtex UltraScale+.

Preferred experience

  • Integrating custom hardware design blocks with other subsystems in AMD Vivado.
  • Working with Compute Express Link (CXL), Universal Verification Methodology (UVM) frameworks or hardware emulation platforms.
  • Debugging hardware in the laboratory and designing hardware and software together for systems with embedded processors.
  • Automating engineering tasks using Python, Tcl, Makefiles and shell scripting.

Working arrangement

  • On-site: Five days a week in Dubai, United Arab Emirates.
  • Right to work: NSATS sponsors the UAE residence visa and obtains the permits required for this role at its own cost, in accordance with the DIFC Employment Law.

How to apply

Use the application form for Senior FPGA Developer, reference NSATS-CS-02, and attach your résumé. Describe relevant FPGA work, your own contribution and how you assessed the result, including the design and verification decisions you made.

For questions about the vacancy or help applying, use Careers enquiries. The DIFC recruitment privacy notice explains how the personal data in an application for this role is handled.
