Geomagnetism demonstration dataset

Demonstration dataset. Values are generated to show the record format and analysis; they are not measurement results. Recorded results are available to qualified parties under NDA.

The Geomagnetism demonstration dataset shows how position information is organised and analysed, from an example run to the individual sensor rows associated with each point. Readers can examine the fields, follow the calculations presented in the charts and inspect individual examples before considering recorded evidence.

The charts and sample rows are available on this page. Downloading the complete demonstration files requires verification of a company email address, without a non-disclosure agreement (NDA). Recorded results are a separate collection, available to qualified parties under a signed NDA.

Explore the demonstration data

The dataset contains 2,431 example runs, comprising 2,426,548 points and 17,001,075 point/sensor rows. A run groups a sequence of points, with a separate row for each represented sensor at each point. The examples include configurations with six, seven or eight sensors; the counts describe the supplied data, not the performance of an implementation.

The charts initially show the complete dataset. The Sensors per run control filters the deviation-range and deviation-distribution charts, while the sensor-configuration chart always shows the full register. Filtering changes the view without altering the supplied values. The linked chart images also show the full dataset and do not change with the filter.

Deviation range for each run

Generated values — not measurement results.

Each vertical line shows the minimum and maximum horizontal deviation supplied for one example run. The range includes its point/sensor rows and retains the start-point zeros. Select a sensor configuration to compare the corresponding examples, or leave All configurations selected to see all 2,431 runs.

Generated values — not measurement results.

Sensor configurations in the run register

Generated values — not measurement results.

The register contains 804 example runs with six sensors, 800 with seven sensors and 827 with eight sensors. These counts cover all 2,431 runs and remain unchanged when the deviation charts are filtered. They describe the composition of the demonstration dataset, not a comparison of positioning performance.

Inspect an example run

Enter a run number to see its identifier, sensor count, number of point/sensor rows and minimum and maximum deviation. The inspector retrieves supplied example values; it does not operate equipment or perform a positioning test.

Horizontal deviation is the distance between the calculated and reference positions at a point. The charts show this distance in metres. Percentage deviation in the complete files divides that distance by the distance between consecutive reference positions, then multiplies by 100. Where the reference step is zero, the percentage is undefined.

How we use your information

NSATS Ltd uses the details you provide to respond to your enquiry and manage the requested discussion. General enquiries are normally retained for 12 months after the last substantive exchange. Relevant contractual records follow their own retention schedule. We do not use your enquiry for unsolicited marketing.

These services are for people aged 18 or over. Privacy notice explains the responsible company, processing locations and your rights. Contact the privacy team.
