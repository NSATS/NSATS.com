NSATS / Enterprise Business Centre

Due-diligence guidance

A due-diligence review starts with the proposed relationship and the decision to be made. Identify the questions that could affect that decision, then distinguish an explanation of the technology from evidence that would support a particular conclusion.

Due diligence is the examination of information relevant to a proposed business relationship before a decision is made.

Technical information

The research and evidence pages for each portfolio explain how to read evidence and relate it to an application. An explanatory resource describes an idea or method; an evidence record documents the basis for a particular finding. Approved evidence records will appear on the relevant research pages, but those records are still pending and are not available. Quantum and Geomagnetism records remain separate.

Under NSATS’s confidentiality procedure, technical information beyond public material is discussed within a licensing enquiry, with an NDA requested through that enquiry where needed. The agreement must be accepted before any exchange that depends on it.
