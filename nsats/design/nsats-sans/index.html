Overview

NSATS Sans is the NSATS typeface for letters, reports and the website. It is a modified version of IBM Plex Sans 3.005, released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by IBM.

Selected letters and figures have been redrawn or refined, and the lowercase made taller, with the aim of making characters easier to distinguish. Version 1.204 is approved for the NSATS website and NSATS documents.

Character set

The 388-character set includes English letters, Western and Central European accented letters, Baltic and Turkish letters, figures, punctuation, symbols and 15 combining accents. Characters outside this set, such as the Romanian comma-below letters Ș, ș, Ț and ț, require a fallback font.

LimitationsCharacters outside the set, such as Ș, ș, Ț and ț, fall back to another font. Word applies kerning only when “Kerning for fonts” is on; since 1.204 this includes Word 2010. With kerning off, the accent of ď ľ ť can touch a following capital T, V, W, X or Y or a closing bracket, as in IBM Plex Sans; with kerning on it keeps clear of every following character. Tests with the 1.202 design, which has the same outlines, found that accent stacks outside the character set could clip at TextEdit’s default line height or overlap the preceding line’s ogoneks in Keynote at 11 pt on 14 pt lines; increased Keynote line spacing was not tested. On the first line of the measured TextEdit PDF, Bold Italic Å and SemiBold Italic ĥ lost at most 0.06 pt, not visibly at the tested sizes. Separately, the five tested accent stacks became unrelated ASCII characters in Preview-saved copies of PDFs exported from Pages, Keynote, TextEdit and Word. PDFium recovered those stacks from the first three applications’ PDFs, but not Word’s. The tested ordinary accented words showed no comparable visual problem and copied correctly, allowing for composed and decomposed forms. These findings concern the tested files, text and settings; 1.204 was not re-measured in these applications.
TestingNative Mac testing used NSATS Clear 1.202, which has the same outlines as 1.204: Word, Pages, Keynote, TextEdit, Preview, Safari, Chrome and Firefox on macOS 26.6.2, at the recorded application versions. On Windows 11, 1.203 was tested in Word 2010, in Edge, Chrome and Firefox (including loading the fonts from this website), in DirectWrite and GDI rendering, in Paint and in print; the first 1.204 candidate was tested for the accent spacing and Word 2010 kerning. The released 1.204 fonts were checked by measurement, by rendering on Linux and by an independent review, not on Windows. Developer kits were tested on Linux only.
Not yet testedThe released 1.204 fonts on Windows and installed on a Mac; iPhone and Android with 1.204; the effect of the code-page settings; reader studies; native-language review; complete clipping coverage. These remain unverified; owner approval does not mean they have passed.
