Scope and delivery

Describe the goods or services, deliverables, acceptance criteria and arrangements for changes. Identify the documents that form part of the agreement.

In a supply agreement, scope describes the work included in the engagement. A reference to another document should identify which document is meant and whether it supplies a specification, a description of work or another part of the agreed requirement. If the description of a deliverable appears in several places, those descriptions should be consistent about what is to be supplied.

Commercial arrangements

Record price, invoicing and payment arrangements, together with any relevant dependencies or conditions.

A price describes the charge for the agreed supply; invoicing arrangements concern how that charge is presented; payment arrangements concern when and on what agreed basis it is paid. A charge may concern the whole engagement or a particular deliverable; its description should identify which supply it relates to.

Information and rights

Address confidentiality, access to information and the rights relevant to work created or used in the engagement. Keep these provisions specific to the proposed supply.

Work may use material that already exists as well as create something new. An agreement should distinguish those categories when describing what each party may use and for what purpose. Permission to receive a document, permission to use its content and responsibility for keeping it confidential address different questions; the relevant arrangements should be clear for the material involved.

Applicable agreement

The terms for an engagement need to identify the contracting entities and address the legal requirements relevant to that relationship.

A trading name used in conversation may differ from the name of the organisation entering an agreement. The agreement should make the parties identifiable and connect each responsibility to the relevant party. The legal requirements that apply need to be established for the actual relationship, including the work and organisations involved.
