Supplier terms and conditions

Version 1.0, effective 1 December 2026. These terms apply to the supply of goods, services and software to NSATS Ltd under a purchase order, through whichever NSATS office places the order.

Application

The purchase order and these terms form the contract between National Scientific AI & Technical Systems Ltd (NSATS Ltd), registered in the Dubai International Financial Centre, and the supplier named in the order. An office placing an order acts on NSATS Ltd’s behalf.

If NSATS Ltd and the supplier have signed an agreement governing the supply, that agreement prevails over these terms to the extent of any inconsistency. The supplier’s own terms do not apply, including terms on quotations, invoices or delivery notes. The supplier accepts the order and these terms by confirming the order or starting to perform it.

Orders and changes

Orders must be in writing and identify the goods, services or software, the price, delivery requirements and the NSATS office placing the order.

Changes to an order must be agreed in writing by NSATS Ltd and the supplier. This includes changes to the scope, price or delivery requirements. NSATS Ltd has no obligation to purchase anything beyond the goods, services or software specified in the order.

Delivery and performance

The supplier must deliver the goods and perform the services on the date and at the place specified in the order. Time is of the essence where the order expressly says so.

Risk in goods passes to NSATS Ltd on delivery. Ownership of goods passes on delivery or payment, whichever occurs first. Intellectual property rights in deliverables are dealt with under “Intellectual property”.

Services must be performed with reasonable skill and care by people suitably qualified and experienced for the work. NSATS Ltd may reject goods or services that do not conform to the order within a reasonable time after delivery or performance. It may require the supplier to replace the goods, perform the services again or refund the price paid for the rejected supply.

Price, invoicing and payment

The price is fixed in the currency stated in the order. It excludes VAT or sales tax but includes all other charges for fulfilling the order.

The supplier must invoice after delivery or, where the order requires acceptance, after acceptance. Each invoice must quote the order number and use the order’s currency. NSATS Ltd will pay within 30 days after receiving a valid invoice issued in accordance with this section.

If an amount is disputed, NSATS Ltd may withhold that amount while the dispute is being resolved. The undisputed balance remains payable within the payment period above.

Warranties

The supplier warrants that goods will conform to the order and its specification, be of satisfactory quality and be fit for the purpose stated in the order. Goods must be free from defects for twelve months from delivery.

The supplier warrants that services will conform to the order and be performed with reasonable skill and care. Software must be supplied as licensed under the licence terms specified in the order and must be free of malicious code.

The supplier also warrants that it will comply with applicable law in performing the order.

Intellectual property

Deliverables created specifically for NSATS Ltd under an order belong to NSATS Ltd on payment. With effect from payment, the supplier assigns to NSATS Ltd all intellectual property rights capable of assignment in those deliverables. The supplier must procure the same assignment from its staff and subcontractors and execute the written assignments needed to give effect to this provision, identifying the deliverables and rights assigned.

The supplier retains ownership of its pre-existing materials. To the extent those materials are included in or needed to use the deliverables, the supplier grants NSATS Ltd a licence, free of charge, to use them for that purpose.

The supplier warrants that the deliverables do not infringe third-party intellectual property rights and must defend NSATS Ltd against claims alleging such infringement.

Without NSATS Ltd’s written consent, the supplier must not use NSATS’s name or marks, or refer to the relationship with NSATS Ltd, in publicity.

Confidentiality

Information received from NSATS Ltd under an order is confidential. The supplier must use it only to perform the order and disclose it only to people who need it for that purpose and are bound by the same confidentiality obligations. On NSATS Ltd’s request, the supplier must return or destroy the information.

These obligations do not apply to information that is publicly available other than through a breach of confidentiality, was already lawfully known to the supplier without a confidentiality restriction, or was independently developed without using NSATS Ltd’s confidential information. The supplier may disclose information where required by law, but only to the extent required.

The confidentiality obligations continue for five years after the order ends. For information that constitutes a trade secret, they continue indefinitely.

Personal data

Before processing personal data on NSATS Ltd’s behalf, the supplier must sign NSATS Ltd’s data-processing terms. It must process that data only on NSATS Ltd’s documented instructions, subject to any processing required by applicable law, and apply appropriate security measures meeting the requirements of applicable data protection law.

The supplier must not transfer personal data out of the jurisdiction specified by NSATS Ltd without NSATS Ltd’s prior written consent. Such consent does not permit a transfer prohibited by applicable law or replace any safeguards that law requires.

The privacy notice governs NSATS Ltd’s own handling of the supplier’s contact data.

Compliance

The supplier must comply with all laws applicable to its performance of the order, including sanctions and export-control requirements, anti-bribery and anti-corruption law, and employment and labour law. It must not make facilitation payments in connection with the order.

NSATS Ltd may terminate the order immediately by written notice if the supplier breaches this section. The period for remedying a material breach under “Termination” does not apply to this right.

Liability

Nothing in the contract excludes or limits either party’s liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, fraud, breach of “Confidentiality”, “Personal data” or “Compliance”, intellectual property infringement, or any liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited.

Subject to those exceptions, each party’s total liability under an order is limited to the price payable under that order. Subject to the same exceptions, neither party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss.

Insurance, records and subcontracting

The supplier must maintain insurance appropriate to the goods, services or software supplied under the order. Where the order specifies insurance amounts, the supplier must maintain cover meeting those requirements.

Records relating to the order must be kept for the period required by law and made available for inspection by NSATS Ltd on reasonable notice.

The supplier must not subcontract the whole of an order without NSATS Ltd’s written consent. It remains responsible for its subcontractors’ performance and compliance with the contract, whether or not consent has been given.

Termination

Either party may terminate an order by written notice if the other commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days after receiving notice of the breach requiring it to be remedied. Either party may also terminate on the other’s insolvency, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

NSATS Ltd may cancel an order for convenience by written notice to the supplier. In that case, NSATS Ltd will pay for conforming goods delivered and services performed up to the date the cancellation takes effect.

Provisions that by their nature are intended to continue after an order ends remain in effect, including the continuing obligations under “Intellectual property” and “Confidentiality”.

General

Neither party is liable for delay caused by events beyond its reasonable control. This does not excuse payment obligations. The affected party must notify the other of the event and its effect on performance and take reasonable steps to mitigate the delay.

The supplier must not assign the contract without NSATS Ltd’s consent. Notices under the contract must be in writing and sent to the addresses stated in the order.

The order, these terms and any signed agreement governing the supply constitute the entire agreement between the parties concerning that supply.

A delay in exercising a contractual right does not waive that right. If a provision is invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in effect to the extent permitted by law. No person other than the parties has a right to enforce the contract.

Governing law

The contract and any dispute arising out of or in connection with it are governed by the laws of the Dubai International Financial Centre. The courts of the Dubai International Financial Centre have exclusive jurisdiction over those disputes.
