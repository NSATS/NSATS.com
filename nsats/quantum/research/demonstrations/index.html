How we use your information

NSATS Ltd uses the details you provide to respond to your enquiry and manage the requested discussion. General enquiries are normally retained for 12 months after the last substantive exchange. Relevant contractual records follow their own retention schedule. We do not use your enquiry for unsolicited marketing.

These services are for people aged 18 or over. Privacy notice explains the responsible company, processing locations and your rights. Contact the privacy team.
