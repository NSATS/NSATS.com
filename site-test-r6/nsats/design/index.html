NSATS Design

NSATS Design brings together the website’s colour themes and the NSATS Sans typeface. It is for people creating NSATS documents, pages and presentations, and developers styling an implementation. Download the colour definitions and font files for use on the NSATS website and in NSATS documents.

NSATS Sans

6 styles, 388 characters, version 1.203

NSATS Sans is the NSATS typeface for letters, reports and the website, developed as a modified version of IBM Plex Sans. Version 1.203 is approved for the NSATS website and NSATS documents, and is available to download.
