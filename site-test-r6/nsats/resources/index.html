Resources

Explore NSATS through its technology explanations, application pages and learning material. Quantum and Geomagnetism have separate resource routes so that each subject can be followed in its own context.

  • Technology and applications

    Technology pages explain what a subject does and why it matters. Application pages connect that subject to a task a licensee could explore. Reading them together helps distinguish the licensed function from the wider system needed to use it.

    Quantum resources →Geomagnetism resources →

  • Guides and evaluation

    Learning routes and glossaries support an introduction to the subjects. Executive guides connect them to development questions, while evaluation pages explain what a useful assessment would need to examine.

    The learning centre offers an application-led starting point alongside routes through the computing and positioning subjects.

    Learning centre →Quantum guide for decision makers →Executive guide to geomagnetic positioning →
