Overview

NSATS Clear is the NSATS typeface for letters, reports and the website. It is a modified version of IBM Plex Sans 3.005, released under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by IBM.

Selected letters and figures have been redrawn or refined, and the lowercase made taller, with the aim of making characters easier to distinguish. Version 1.100 RC04 is a test release, not approved for production use.

Character set

The 234-character set includes English letters, Western European accented letters, figures, punctuation and symbols. Characters outside this set, such as ł, ő and č, require a fallback font.
