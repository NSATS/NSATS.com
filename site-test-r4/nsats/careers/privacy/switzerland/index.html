Swiss recruitment privacy notice

This notice explains how NSATS SA processes the personal data of people who apply for a role based in Switzerland or contact NSATS about such a role. In this notice, “applicants” includes all of these people. NSATS SA processes this data in accordance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), Article 328b of the Swiss Code of Obligations and, where it applies, the data protection law of the European Union. NSATS SA will comply with all national and other regulations that apply to its recruitment at any time.

Applicants for roles based in the DIFC, and people with a careers enquiry that does not concern a particular role, should read the DIFC recruitment privacy notice.

Who is responsible

NSATS SA, a company registered in Switzerland, is the controller of the personal data covered by this notice.

Postal address: PO 0011, Cornavin, Geneva 1201, Switzerland.

NSATS SA has appointed its own data protection officer. Questions and requests about this notice, and messages for NSATS SA’s data protection officer, should be made through the privacy enquiries form, choosing NSATS SA, or sent by post to the address above, marked for the attention of the Data Protection Officer.

Privacy enquiries →

Why NSATS SA processes applicants’ data

NSATS SA processes applicants’ personal data to assess their suitability for the vacancy applied for, to answer their enquiries, to communicate with them about their application, to take steps at their request before entering into an employment contract, and to meet its legal obligations. In line with Article 328b of the Swiss Code of Obligations, it processes only data that concerns the applicant’s suitability for the role.

The legal obligations include the check required by Article 91 of the Federal Act on Foreign Nationals and Integration that a foreign national is authorised to work in Switzerland, and, where a refused applicant who claims discrimination requests one, a written statement of reasons under Article 8 of the Federal Act on Gender Equality.

What NSATS SA collects

NSATS SA collects only the personal data needed to assess an applicant’s suitability for the role or to answer an enquiry: contact details, the vacancy reference, the content of any enquiry, the information about experience and qualifications that the applicant provides, any supporting material the applicant chooses to share, notes made during the recruitment process and, for the successful applicant, the information needed to confirm the right to work. NSATS SA does not ask about health, religion, family circumstances or finances unless the law requires it, and asks applicants not to include such information, or other sensitive information that the role does not require, in their application.

Providing personal data is voluntary. NSATS SA cannot consider an application without the contact details, vacancy reference and relevant experience requested in the application form, or answer an enquiry without a means of reply. The successful applicant must provide the information needed to confirm the right to work, because the law requires it; without it, NSATS SA cannot employ them.

Who receives the data

Applicants’ personal data is seen only by the people at NSATS SA involved in the recruitment decision or in answering the enquiry, and by service providers that host NSATS SA’s recruitment systems in Switzerland under contract and on NSATS SA’s instructions. For the successful applicant, NSATS SA gives the information needed for a work or residence permit to the Swiss authorities that issue it. Applicants’ personal data is not shared with NSATS Ltd in the DIFC. NSATS SA does not sell applicants’ personal data or use it for direct marketing.

Where the data is held

The personal data covered by this notice is stored and processed in Switzerland and is not disclosed abroad. If a disclosure abroad ever becomes necessary, NSATS SA will make it only as the FADP permits, with the guarantees it requires, and will update this notice before the disclosure takes place.

How long NSATS SA keeps the data

NSATS SA keeps the personal data of unsuccessful applicants for six months after the recruitment decision, and the personal data in an enquiry that does not lead to an application for six months after NSATS SA replies, and then deletes it. If a legal claim about the recruitment decision or the enquiry has been made, NSATS SA keeps the data needed for that claim until the claim is resolved, and then deletes it. The personal data of the successful applicant becomes part of their employment record.

Automated decisions

NSATS SA does not make recruitment decisions based solely on automated processing and does not use artificial intelligence tools to assess applications.

Applicants’ rights

Subject to the conditions of the FADP, applicants may ask whether NSATS SA processes their personal data and receive a copy of it, ask for incorrect data to be corrected, ask for a processing activity or a disclosure to be stopped or for their data to be deleted, and, where the conditions are met, receive the personal data they provided in a conventional electronic format. Requests should be made through the privacy enquiries form. NSATS SA provides this information free of charge, generally within 30 days, except where the FADP provides otherwise.

Applicants may report a concern to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC). Where the data protection law of the European Union applies, applicants also have the rights it gives and may complain to the data protection authority of the country in which they live.
