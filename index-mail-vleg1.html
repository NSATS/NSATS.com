Key features

Unlimited storage (no fair-use limitation on storage)

Mailbox capacity is unlimited and not subject to any fair-use limitation on stored email or attachments. Storage is provided without quota monitoring or forced deletion. This storage policy does not modify acceptable-use controls for sending activity, spam prevention, or abuse enforcement.

Encrypted connections (SSL/TLS)

Access to NSATS Mail is protected using SSL/TLS encrypted connections to secure credentials and mailbox traffic in transit between user devices and NSATS Mail systems.

Post-quantum security architecture

Post-quantum indicates an architecture designed to adopt cryptographic methods intended to remain robust as future computing capabilities evolve, reducing dependence on a single generation of cryptographic assumptions.
