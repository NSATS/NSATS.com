Engineering & System Design

NSATS provides professional engineering and system design services under Class 42 for third parties, including advice and consultancy relating to the design of computer hardware, advisory services relating to computer systems design, computer system analysis, computer network design for others, and design and development of telecommunications networks and communications systems.

Representative Services Provided

Next: Web & Platform →

The items below are representative of engineering and system design services offered by NSATS to enterprise and organizational clients. Services are provided as professional design, consultancy, and technical analysis engagements for third parties.

  • Advice relating to the design of computer hardware and advisory services relating to computer hardware design.
  • Advisory services relating to computer systems design and consultancy in the field of technological design.
  • Computer system analysis, computer analysis, and comparative studies of efficiency or performance of computer systems.
  • Computer network design for others, including network architecture planning for enterprise environments.
  • Design and development of telecommunications networks and design of communication systems for organizational infrastructures.
  • Design of information technology infrastructure for others, including integration of computer systems and networks.
  • Computer specification design and technical planning for systems for data input, output, processing, display, and storage.
  • Engineering services relating to computers, including feasibility study services relating to computers and technology environments.
  • Computer performance testing and design and development of testing and analysis methods for computing systems.
  • IT consultancy, advisory and information services relating to system integration and infrastructure planning.
Commercial delivery: Engineering and system design services are delivered through commissioned consulting engagements, architecture documentation, engineering specifications, design studies, feasibility reports, and implementation planning for third-party organizations.

Engineering & System Design Service Catalog

This catalog summarizes common engineering and system design activities performed by NSATS. Items may be combined into a single engagement and tailored to third-party environments, constraints, and technical objectives.

Computer systems architecture and specification design

System architecture planning, requirements definition support, and computer specification design for input, output, processing, storage, and display needs.

Computer system analysis and performance assessment

Computer system analysis, comparative efficiency studies, bottleneck identification, and performance recommendations aligned to operational requirements.

Computer network design for others

Network topology design, segmentation planning, routing and connectivity architecture, and integration planning for enterprise networks.

Telecommunications and communications system design studies

Design studies and technical planning for telecommunications networks and communication systems, including constraint-driven deployment planning.

Hardware advisory and infrastructure planning

Advisory services relating to computer hardware design, component selection support, and infrastructure planning for reliability and performance.

Test planning and analysis method design

Design and development of testing and analysis methods, including performance test plans and validation approaches for computing environments.

Feasibility studies and engineering assessments

Feasibility study services relating to computers and networks, including trade-off analyses and implementation planning recommendations.

System integration planning

Integration of computer systems and networks through documented architecture options, dependency mapping, and integration sequencing plans.

Typical Deliverables

Engineering and system design engagements commonly result in professional documentation and specifications that support implementation by the client or a third-party implementer. Deliverables vary by scope.

Architecture & design specifications

System diagrams, component specifications, integration plans, and design documentation for enterprise computing environments.

Network & communications designs

Network topology plans, segmentation models, routing approaches, and communications system design documentation.

Hardware advisory outputs

Hardware specification notes, infrastructure planning recommendations, and evaluation guidance for equipment selection.

Technical reports & feasibility findings

Feasibility reports, performance analysis summaries, risk considerations, and implementation planning recommendations.

Note: Where appropriate, deliverables may include test plans, acceptance criteria, integration checklists, and documentation intended to support maintainable operation and change management.

Engagement Models

NSATS provides engineering and system design services through engagement formats aligned to organizational needs, timelines, and delivery constraints.

Common engagement types

  • Architecture assessment and system analysis — review of the current state, technical findings, and design recommendations.
  • Greenfield systems and network design — end-to-end design from requirements through specifications and implementation planning.
  • Telecommunications design study — communications system design planning and documentation aligned to deployment constraints.
  • Hardware advisory engagement — specification design support and planning services for compute, storage, and network equipment.
  • Testing and analysis method design — design of evaluation methods and performance test plans for computing systems.
