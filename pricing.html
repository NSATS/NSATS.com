Pricing

NSATS Mail is offered as a subscription service. All plans provide the same core service features, including unlimited mailbox storage with no fair-use limitation on stored email or attachments, SSL/TLS encrypted access, post-quantum security architecture, IMAP support, spam filtering, and DKIM/DMARC sender verification.

Payments are accepted via major credit and debit cards. Subscription charges are processed securely at the start of each billing term. Plans renew for the selected term unless cancelled prior to renewal. Account access remains active while a subscription is in force.

Billing notes

What is included

All plans include unlimited mailbox storage (no fair-use limitation on storage), SSL/TLS encrypted access, post-quantum security architecture, IMAP, spam filtering, and DKIM/DMARC.

Storage versus sending policy

Unlimited storage applies to retained email and attachments. Acceptable-use and anti-abuse controls remain in force for sending behavior, spam prevention, and account protection.

Short term
