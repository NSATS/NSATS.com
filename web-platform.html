Web & Platform Design/Development

NSATS provides professional web and platform design and development services under Class 42, including creating and designing web pages for others, creating and maintaining websites, designing and implementing network web pages, electronic site authoring, database design and development, and constructing internet platforms for electronic commerce.

Representative Services Provided

Next: Software →

The services below are representative examples of web and platform design/development services offered by NSATS to business and organizational clients. Services are provided as commercial design, development, maintenance, and consultancy engagements.

  • Computer website design, including web site design consultancy and web site design and creation services.
  • Creating and designing web pages for others and designing and developing webpages on the internet.
  • Creating, designing and maintaining web sites and building and maintaining websites for third parties.
  • Creating and updating of home pages for computer networks and development, design and updating of home pages.
  • Creating electronically stored web pages for online services and the internet and electronic site authoring.
  • Constructing an internet platform for electronic commerce and hosting of e-commerce platforms on the Internet (where applicable).
  • Design, creation and programming of web pages, including commissioned writing of code for creation of web pages.
  • Design and development of electronic database software, including database design and development and design of computer databases.
  • Design and development of online websites for third parties, including ongoing maintenance and updating of websites.
Commercial delivery: NSATS delivers services through scoped project engagements, including requirements capture, UX/UI design, software implementation, integration, testing, deployment support, and ongoing maintenance and updating for third parties.

Service Catalog

This catalog summarizes the types of web and platform design/development services offered by NSATS. Items are representative and may be combined within a single engagement.

Website design and creation

Design and creation of websites for third parties, including information sites, corporate sites, and service portals.

Web application and platform development

Design and development of web applications and online platforms, including custom interfaces and backend services.

E-commerce platform construction

Constructing internet platforms for electronic commerce, including product catalogs, checkout workflows, and integrations.

Homepage and network page development

Creating, updating, and maintaining home pages and network web pages for organizations and third parties.

Electronic site authoring and content structuring

Electronic site authoring, structured page creation, and creation of electronically stored web pages for online services.

Database design and integration

Database design and development, electronic database software design where required, and integration planning for web systems.

Maintenance and updating for third parties

Design maintenance, ongoing updates, modernization, performance improvements, and technical support for web properties.

Typical Deliverables

NSATS engagements commonly result in professional documentation and implemented web assets supporting deployment and ongoing maintenance.

UX/UI design and page templates

Page layouts, user flows, interface mockups, component specifications, and accessibility considerations aligned to requirements.

Implemented pages and platform components

Developed web pages, platform modules, authentication flows, and integration endpoints required for organizational use cases.

Database and integration specifications

Data models, database schema drafts, integration requirements, and API specifications supporting platform features.

Maintenance plan and update scope

Update schedule, modernization plans, content update procedures, and performance and security maintenance recommendations.

Engagement Models

NSATS provides web and platform design/development services through structured engagements tailored to client requirements, timelines, and operational needs.

Common engagement types

  • Website design and build — design, creation, and deployment support for a website built for third parties.
  • Platform development engagement — multi-module web platform design and development with authentication, dashboards, and integrations.
  • E-commerce platform construction — building an online commerce platform, including workflows and integration planning.
  • Homepage and network page program — creation, updating, and maintenance of home pages and network pages for organizations.
  • Modernization and maintenance — ongoing updates, page refresh, performance improvements, and technical maintenance for third parties.
