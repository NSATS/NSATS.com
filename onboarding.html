What Happens Next

  • Intake review: NSATS reviews the submission and confirms the appropriate service category and point of contact.
  • Scope clarification: A brief consultation call or written clarification to confirm objectives, constraints, and deliverables.
  • Engagement definition: Preparation of a statement of work or service proposal describing scope, timelines, and delivery approach.
  • Kickoff: Engagement start, with deliverables and reporting aligned to the agreed scope.

This onboarding process supports NSATS delivery of professional services to third-party organizations through commissioned projects, consulting engagements, managed service arrangements, and technical advisory work.

Typical Deliverables

Deliverables vary by service category and engagement model. The examples below are representative of professional outputs delivered for third parties.

  • Design documentation: architecture diagrams, system specifications, integration plans, and technical design notes.
  • Software outputs: prototype implementations, code deliverables, integration components, and testing plans.
  • Operational artifacts: runbooks, monitoring dashboards/alerts (where applicable), operational procedures, and support documentation.
  • Technical reports: feasibility studies, benchmarking summaries, risk assessments, and implementation guidance.
  • Deployment and migration plans: rollout sequencing, environment setup guidance, and transition planning for third-party teams.
