What should I include in an introduction?

Describe the goods or services, relevant experience, delivery capability and business contact. Explain the specific contribution you are proposing.

How should I present several capabilities?

Separate distinct areas of supply. Give each a clear scope and identify the resources or dependencies relevant to it.

What information helps with an invoice enquiry?

Provide your organisation name, invoice number, relevant order or agreement reference and a short description of the question.

The invoicing page also covers preparing an invoice and the payment arrangements behind a query.

Invoicing and payment enquiries →

How should I report a change to supplier details?

Identify the record and the type of update through the supplier enquiry route. Sensitive changes need a verified process.

Update supplier details →

How does qualification differ from an introduction?

An introduction explains what a supplier could contribute. Qualification examines the information and conditions relevant to a particular engagement.
